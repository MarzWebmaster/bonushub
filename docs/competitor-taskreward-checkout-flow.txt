TASKREWARD: Purchase Flow Analysis — Checkout UI Example

## Pakej Contoh: 40-50 LIKE + COMMENT TIKTOK (COMBO) 🔥

### Checkout Fields:
1. **Link (pilihan)** — Pautan untuk link tugasan yang dijana
2. **Nota Pembeli** — Dipaparkan kepada pekerja tugasan

### Pengesahan Sebelum Pembelian:
⚠️ Amaran: Semua pakej (followers, likes, views, komen) tidak dijamin kekal — boleh berkurang/hilang dari semasa ke semasa. Kerana platform (TikTok, IG, FB, Threads) ada sistem penapisan & pembersihan akaun automatik.

### Harga Breakdown:
| Item | Harga |
|------|-------|
| Harga Pakej | RM6.00 |
| ToyyibPay Fee | RM1.00 |
| **Jumlah** | **RM7.00** |

### Payment Options:
- Baki Ganjaran: RM0.00
- Baki Topup: RM0.00

---

## KEY INSIGHTS UNTUK BONUSHUB

### 1. UI/UX Pattern:
- Simple form: Link + Nota → Harga → Bayar
- Fee jelas terpisah (ToyyibPay fee RM1)
- 2 payment source: Ganjaran (earned) + Topup (deposited)

### 2. Business Model:
- Harga rendah (RM6-7) = volume tinggi
- Fee RM1 per transaction = revenue tambahan
- Pakej combo (Like+Comment) = value bundling

### 3. Legal/Compliance:
- ✅ Amaran jelas tentang risiko (tidak dijamin kekal)
- ✅ Transparent pricing (fee terpisah)
- ✅ Pengesahan sebelum pembelian
- ✅ 2 payment methods (flexibility)

### 4. Technical Implementation Notes:
- Link validation (TikTok URL detection)
- Nota field (instructions untuk workers)
- Dual balance system (Ganjaran + Topup)
- Fee calculation (ToyyibPay integration)
- Order confirmation flow

### 5. Differentiation for BonusHub:
- BonusHub: Merchant-centric (merchant creates tasks)
- TaskReward: User-centric (user buys engagement)
- BonusHub: Points/rewards based (no cash deposit)
- TaskReward: Cash-based (FPX topup)

### 6. Compliance Notes:
- Amaran risiko = good practice (consumer protection)
- Fee transparency = comply with Consumer Protection Act 1999
- Payment methods = comply with BNM guidelines
- Data privacy = comply with PDPA 2010
